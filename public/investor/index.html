The problem

Hiring a caregiver is stressful for families, let alone having a consistent caregiver.

Studies show that consistent caregiving provides a lasting foundation for emotional development. Our countries are aging. Who will take care of our children and our elders?

Care that restarts

Each departure returns the family to the beginning: new search, new trust, new routine for the child.

Care that compounds

One caregiver who stays builds the familiarity a child grows on, and the household stops starting over.

Why cross-border, why Indonesia

Indonesia is the world's #2 largest domestic worker and caregiver exporter.

We build the supply pool then open to families all over the world.

Indonesia Singapore Malaysia Hong Kong Japan Middle East
The product

Four systems. One relationship.

Each layer runs on its own product, and every product feeds the next.

01 NUOS Match

AI vetting and matching before a family ever meets a caregiver.

02 NUOS Academy

Learn to earn training that raises wages with skill.

03 NUOS Partner

Ongoing monitoring, payroll, and conflict resolution.

04 NUOS Tumbuh

A shared, evidence based view of the child's development.

Together, they make a cross-border hire as safe as one next door.

Revenue model

A business built to earn on quality, not just placement.

Aligning incentives with families, preserving long-term relationships with caregivers.

Why now

Starting with childcare, then rolling out to elderly care. Indonesia alone has 11 million families and a ~$3.6B serviceable market for childcare, with hundreds of billions worldwide.
